`exact`: same street key and number · `partial`: source street is part of the EMUiA name ("Kutrzeby" → "Generała Tadeusza Kutrzeby") · `fuzzy`: rapidfuzz ≥ 85 · `~number`: the exact number is not in EMUiA, the plain number is used ("1A" → "1") · `rspo+emuia`: house number from the national school register RSPO (same street as the city record), coordinates from EMUiA · `nominatim+emuia`: house number from OSM, coordinates from EMUiA · `osm+emuia`: the same institution in OSM (matching type, number or name, street), placed on the nearest EMUiA address point of the source street (≤ 60 m) · `manual`: `manual_fixes.csv`.
